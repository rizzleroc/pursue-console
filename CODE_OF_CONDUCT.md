# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be kind. Disagree about evidence, not about people. This is a topic that attracts strong opinions; we keep the focus on the documents.

## What we expect

- Discuss claims, not contributors.
- Cite primary sources.
- Assume good faith in pull requests and reviews.
- Welcome people who are new to the codebase, the topic, or open source.
- Speculation belongs in your own writing, not in this repo's data files.

## What's not acceptable

- Personal attacks, harassment, doxxing, or threats — in issues, PRs, commits, or anywhere associated with the project.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Posting other contributors' private information.
- Bad-faith spam: drive-by accusations, low-effort copy-paste, conspiracy-baiting unrelated to the documents.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and PRs that violate this code, and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently.

To report a violation, open an issue tagged `conduct` or email the repo owner directly via their GitHub profile. Reports are confidential.

This document is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
