Map of Creature Sightings from Approximately Three-Mile Radius around the Ranch A Google Earth map of the ranch and surr, ounding areas was created (approx altitude of map was from 30,000 feet). The locations of each creature sighting together with last known direction of travel were obtained from each eyewitness and then plotted on the map. Then, using an assumption of straight line travel (extrapolation of known direction of movement of creatures), a series of straight lines were drawn on the map. The red circles represent intersection points of these lines. In a sense, these intersection points could be construed as arbitrary, particularly since it is recognized that the creatures may have changed their direction of travel once out of sight of the eyewitnesses. Nevertheless, with the above caveats in mind, it is noteworthy that there are three intersection points of the creatures' putative lines of travel. It can be seen from the figure that all three convergence points are very close to the ranch. One intersection point lies just west of the ranch and the other two lie in the vicinity of Bottle Hollow reservoir. If locations for sustained and continuous night watching to capture sightings/photographs of these creatures were to be nominated, the three locations delineated by the red circles could be top of the list. 5

Utah Ranch Report Abstract The "Utah Ranch" investigation is an ongoing project which to date has incorporated several deployments of investigators, scientists and engineers. The primary focus of the Utah ranch investigations are: • To comprehensively interview a large number of people who have lived in the area surrounding the Utah Ranch in order to delineate the scope and variety of eyewitness reports in the area. The purpose is to establish commonalities and differences with the ongoing reports of unusual activity on the ranch property itself. • To use portable sensor equipment to measure baseline magnetic field parameters, EM spectrum data, ionizing radiation and chemical elemental analysis of soil on the Utah Ranch property and surrounding areas. It goes without saying a primary purpose is to obtain definitive photographic, digital, sound and chemical evidence of purported anomalies. • To use plants, including transgenic plants, as biosensors of multiple radiation and magnetic field deviations from the norm on the ranch property. These plant experiments have been tailored as user friendly reporting systems for electromagnetic energy detection based on extensive research of the peer reviewed literature. • To initiate a variety of games, puzzles, and other strategies in order to establish communication with the source/origin of reported anomalies on the ranch property. Witnesses have reported unidentified balls of light at close range, daytime sightings of "dog-like" creatures running on two legs, unnatural animalistic howling/screaming, "little people", prehistoric-like birds and a maggot which measured about 8-feet long and 2 to 3-feet in diameter. To date, May 7, 2010, over twenty-five (25) people have been contacted and interviewed in person, predominantly at their homes. This group consists of all life-long residents of the area within a mile or two of the ranch property, four (4) local police officers, eleven (11) Native Americas, including a Shaman, the director of the local Fish and Wildlife office, twelve (12) ranchers, six (6) business owners and approximately fifteen (15) members of the Mormon Church.
