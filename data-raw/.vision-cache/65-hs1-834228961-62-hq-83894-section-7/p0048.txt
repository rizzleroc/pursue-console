[Handwritten note:] OF FLYING SAUCERS

Dear Mr. Hoover: -

Since reading so much about flying saucers and other objects in the sky, I have decided to write you about a theory which I came to believe in 1917

One evening about 10 - I saw an object in the South western sky. It seemed to be falling toward the Earth at about a 50° angle which would let it hit about south of us. It went behind some buildings and when I ran out to see where it might hit, it was moving horizontally with the Earth's surface. Almost immediately it started to rise and seemed to rise faster and
