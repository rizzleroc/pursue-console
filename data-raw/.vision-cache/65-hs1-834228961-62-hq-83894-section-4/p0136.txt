SOUTHWEST - TIMES RECORD
Ft. Smith, Arkansas
April 17, 1949


**Local Man Reports Seeing What He Believes Was "Flying Disc"**

Wade Harrison, Fort Smith post office employe, reported Saturday he had observed what he believed was a "flying disc."
Harrison saw what he believed was a "flying disc" as he was driving east on Grand avenue at Eleventh street. He said the object was small and was moving southeastward at a rapid rate of speed. He said the object resembled a tiny mirror.
There were no other reports of "flying discs" in the area Saturday.
