FEDERAL BUREAU OF INVESTIGATION
COMMUNIST INFILTRATION - COMMUNIST PARTY, MICHIGAN
(NATIONAL INTELLIGENCE)

On April 11, 1960, a letter to Office of Special Investigation, dated April 11, 1960, was [illegible].

On April 11, 1960, [Name] was personally contacted by SA [Name] and SA [Name].

He was advised that the letter dated March 15, 1960, had been referred to the Department of Justice and the FBI acknowledging receipt of that letter.

He was advised that the contents of his letter had been noted and that he had voluntarily turned over the photographs to the Office of Special Investigation, Department of the Air Force, Detroit, Michigan, and that the FBI had been furnished a copy of the letter to the Detroit [illegible].

He was advised that the FBI was taking no investigative action in this matter and he was advised of the Bureau's policy in matters of this nature.

He expressed his appreciation for the cooperation of the FBI.
