July 17, 1963

Mr. John Black
Post Office Box
Sacramento, California

Dear Mr. Black:

Your letter dated July 10, 1963, has been received.

For your information, the investigation of unidentified flying objects does not come within the scope of the investigations of the Federal Bureau of Investigation. A copy of your letter to the Attorney General, Washington, D. C., has been referred to this Bureau for such assistance as we might be able to render.

Sincerely yours,

John Edgar Hoover
Director

NOTE: Copy of incoming correspondence in files not classified. Refer to FBI.
