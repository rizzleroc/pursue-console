Dear Miss McClaskey:

Your letter dated April 4, 1950, has been received and I want to thank you for furnishing me the data you related.

I am taking the liberty of referring a copy of your communication to the Secretary of the Air Force, National Defense Building, The Pentagon, Washington 25, D. C.

Sincerely yours,

John Edgar Hoover
Director

NOTE: [Handwritten circle] Security Division, Espionage Unit, advised. "Flying Saucer" data is being referred to the Air Force.
