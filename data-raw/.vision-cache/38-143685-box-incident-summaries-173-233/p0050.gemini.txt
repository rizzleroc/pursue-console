Incident: 195

On 31 Oct 1948 at 1045 Z, GCA Operator at Goose Bay, Labrador
noticed a target on PPI Scope Number 1. It (the object) supe red
to be about 3 miles south of the field and was traveling on a
southwest heading. It went into a ground clutter at 6 miles.
The blip traveled at a speed of approximately 25 MPH. This
target sighting was verified by Base Commander C. D. Jones and by
Group Commander Captain Verner, who were in the unit at the time.
