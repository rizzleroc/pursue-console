On August 18, 1956, [redacted] a [redacted] at [redacted] Albuquerque, New Mexico, advised that he had been contacted by a group of people who claimed to be from the F.B.I. and who were investigating [redacted] in New Mexico.

[redacted] stated that the code of 23:00 was a code of the F.B.I. and [redacted] stated that this was in the opinion of [redacted] [redacted] and [redacted].

A copy of this communication is being forwarded to Albuquerque office for purposes of information.
