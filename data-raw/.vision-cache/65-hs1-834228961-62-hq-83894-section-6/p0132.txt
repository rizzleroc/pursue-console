SUBJECT: UNKNOWN FLYING OBJECTS
INFORMATION CONCERNING

J. H. KUHN, U.S. Navy, temporarily residing at 1500 Crystal Lane, Nashville, Tennessee, advised CHARLES A. HORTON, Agent, on March 13, 1952, at approximately 5:15 p.m., of the following incident:

Mr. KUHN stated that approximately 10:50 p.m. on March 13, 1952, while standing in the back yard of 1500 Crystal Lane, looking toward the east, he saw an object which was then reduced in size of the section he observed.

Mr. KUHN described that object as being circular in shape, approximately 20 degrees above the horizon.

He stated the object had a reddish tint on the rim and a semi-oval size of the deep light blue color. The object was moving from the southwest. He stated that the object was not in his vision more than ten seconds. He stated that at the time he observed this, it made no sound. J. H. KUHN stated that the stars were out in the sky and there were no clouds in the moon. It [illegible] that J. H. KUHN stated he could have believed this could have been a spotlight on a cloud but he had seen no such lights or [illegible] lights near that vicinity because he had seen no [illegible] during the evening.

The above is being furnished for your information and action in connection with the [illegible] of this office.
