UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT
MEMORANDUM

DATE: July 29, 1952

SUBJECT: FLYING SAUCERS

Reference is made to the memorandum of the Director dated July 24, 1952, concerning the collection of information on flying saucers in the United States.

DETAILS:

Representatives of the [illegible] have requested that the Bureau advise all field offices to forward to the Bureau any information concerning flying saucers reported to the field offices.
